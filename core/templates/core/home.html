Best Time to Trek

October–November offers crystal-clear skies and stunning Himalayan views — peak season for a reason.

Permits You Need

A TIMS card and national park entry permit are required for most major treks. Get them in Kathmandu or Pokhara.

Altitude Sickness

Acclimatise properly above 3,000 m — ascend no more than 500 m per day and take rest days when needed.

What to Pack

Layer your clothing, bring a quality sleeping bag rated to −10°C for tea-house treks, and pack light rain gear.

Currency Tips

Carry Nepali Rupees in smaller denominations for remote areas. ATMs are scarce beyond district headquarters.

Respect Local Culture

Remove shoes before entering temples, dress modestly at religious sites, and always ask before photographing locals.

Best Destinations for Every Season

Nepal has something extraordinary year-round — find your perfect window

Spring

Mar – May

Rhododendron forests in full bloom, moderate temperatures and clear mornings. Ideal for mid-altitude treks.

Annapurna Circuit Poon Hill Langtang
Monsoon

Jun – Aug

Lush green valleys and waterfalls. Rain-shadow regions north of the Himalayas stay dry and crowd-free.

Upper Mustang Dolpo Chitwan Safari
Autumn

Sep – Nov

Crystal-clear skies, stable weather and peak mountain views. The best season for high-altitude expeditions.

Everest Base Camp Langtang Manaslu Circuit
Winter

Dec – Feb

Snow-capped peaks and fewer crowds. Lower elevations and cultural tours offer a peaceful winter escape.
